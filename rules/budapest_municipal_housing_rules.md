# Demo: Budapest Municipal Housing Authority Regulations

## Decree No. 14/2021 (V. 12.) on Municipal Tenancy Protections

### Section 4: Utility Indexation Adjustments
1. Any building-wide utility maintenance fee increase must be announced in writing at least 30 calendar days before the effective date.
2. The tenant has a statutory right to contest any utility increase within 14 calendar days of receiving written notice.
3. If the tenant contests the increase in writing within 14 calendar days, the dispute is referred to the District Notary Office (Jegyzo) for administrative mediation.
4. Failure to contest within 14 calendar days or sign the amendment annex by the stated deadline results in automatic acceptance under Section 9 of the standard tenancy framework.

### Section 8: Late Submission and Administrative Penalties
1. Surcharges for late document submission may not exceed 3,000 HUF per billing cycle unless approved by the Municipal Assembly.
2. Inquiries and formal disputes must be directed to the Budapest Metropolitan Housing Authority Leasing Division (leasing@budapesthousing.hu) or the competent District Mayor Office.
